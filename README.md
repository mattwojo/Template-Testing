# Service-Level Agreement for PR response and resolution

SLAs are based on priority with the following estimates.

| Priority | PR size + Review time (SLA) |
| --- | --- |
| P0	| Small (1-3 files): Same day (~1-4 hours) |
| P0	| Medium (3-6 files): Same day (~4-6 hours) |
| P0	|	Large (6+ files): 1-3 business days |
| P1	| Small (1-3 files): Same day (~4-8 hours) |
| P1	| Medium (3-6 files): 1-2 business days |
| P1	| Large (6+ files): 3-4 business days |
| P2	| Small (1-3 files): 1-2 business days |
| P2	| Medium (3-6 files): 3-4 business days |
| P2	| Large (6+ files): 5-7 business days |
| P3	| Small (1-3 files): 3-4 business days |
| P3	| Medium (3-6 files): 5-7 business days |
| P3	| Large (6+ files):  8-10 business days |

SLAs are based on time to respond with an initial review, not time to resolve and merge the PR. Resolution typically requires collaboration between the individual submitting the PR and the writer, who is responsible for providing an initial review and feedback that upholds Microsoft Learn quality expectations, and maintaining the article freshness and accuracy over time.

## PR review workflow 

1. When submitting a Pull Request, please include a title and description defining the intent of the contribution, along with the type of change and a requested priority level.
2. A writer from the Microsoft Learn content organization will triage the PR, confirming who is assigned to review (ms.author) and the priority level (asking clarifying questions if necessary).
3. The assigned writer will aim to complete the review within the time associated with the PR priority level (sometimes this will be a quicker turn-around or require negotiation depending on availability and resourcing).
4. Once the review is completed, the writer will reply to the alias submitting the PR with feedback (either in the PR comments or via Teams chat) and change the triage tag from "action-required-from-writer" to "action-required-from-contributor" to indicate that the review has been completed and is now waiting on a response resolving or dismissing any feedback. Sometimes feedback resolution will require some back-and-forth collaboration.
5. Once any feedback is resolved, the writer will confirm that the PR is ready to be merged and sign-off to trigger publishing to the live site. (Depending on the amount of change, this may trigger a second vendor team review, typically with a turn-around of a couple of hours.)

Publishing times for this repository to the live, public, Microsoft Learn site are (in Pacific Standard Time): 4:00am , 10:00am, 3:00pm, and 10:00pm on every day except for Saturday.
